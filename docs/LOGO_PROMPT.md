## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished professional logo for AP Food, a campus food ordering app. A simple custom AP letter monogram only: an uppercase A and uppercase P, very clearly readable as AP. Use a narrow, elegant kitchen-knife silhouette as the straight vertical stem of the P; integrate the P's bowl cleanly into the upper blade/stem, so it remains a letter first and subtle culinary reference second. The A is a solid, carefully drawn geometric uppercase with a small triangular counter; the two letters share an intentional baseline and optical weight, compact balanced composition. Restrained espresso brown #3B2A1E for the entire mark, a tiny mustard gold #D3A10B accent may be used only on the knife handle. Think credible independent food brand designed by a skilled human identity designer: simple flat vector-like shapes, precise edges, strong silhouette, tasteful proportions, recognisable at favicon size, restrained negative space. Center one logo in a square canvas with generous empty margin on a genuinely transparent background. No extra text, no wordmark, no grid, no presentation board, no mockup, no paper, no photographic texture, no 3D, no gradients, no shadows, no sparkles, no cartoon chef, no plate, no extra cutlery, no ornamental swirls, no stock-logo flourish. The only letters are A and P. Single clean logo asset, suitable for a real app header.
